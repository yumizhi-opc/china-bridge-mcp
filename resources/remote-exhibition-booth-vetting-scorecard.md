# China Remote Exhibition Booth Vetting Scorecard

Field checklist used during China With Me Remote Exhibition Viewer assignments across 121+ verified trade shows (Canton Fair, CHTF Shenzhen, DMP, CIOE, CHINAPLAS, electronica South China).

See the full redacted deliverable format: https://chinawithme.com/samples/remote-exhibition-report  
Browse upcoming verified China expos: https://chinawithme.com/exhibitions/

## 1. Pre-Show Target Matrix
- [ ] Confirm hall, zone, and booth numbers (note Canton Fair Phases 1–3 transitions).
- [ ] Group booths physically by hall aisle to maximize qualified interviews per floor hour.
- [ ] Lock client engineering tolerances, target certifications (CE/FCC/UL/RoHS/Food Grade), target MOQ, and target landed price ceiling.

## 2. On-Site Booth Verification (Factory vs. Trading Intermediary)
- [ ] Request Chinese mainland entity name (营业执照全称) and check Unified Social Credit Code (`gsxt.gov.cn`) business scope.
- [ ] Verify whether registered scope includes **制造 / 生产 / 加工** (Manufacturing) vs. solely **贸易 / 批发** (Trading).
- [ ] Ask 3 floor-specific technical questions in Mandarin (machinery count, mold workshop city/district, current bottleneck lead times) that catalog salespeople cannot improvise.
- [ ] Photograph factory location on license alongside hall booth placard and sales engineer's WeChat QR card.

## 3. Live Video Walk & Physical Inspection Capture
- [ ] Establish continuous live Zoom or WeChat video stream for key booths so client engineering can steer questions in real time.
- [ ] Film macro close-ups of surface finishes, fasteners, PCBA cleanliness, packaging barcodes, and compliance nameplate stamps (unscripted, continuous 4K clip).

## 4. Sample Tagging, Direct Quotes & Consolidation
- [ ] Collect original factory PDF quotation sheets showing EXW vs. FOB Shenzhen/Ningbo breakdown, tooling NRE, and sample fee refund terms.
- [ ] Tag collected booth samples with Booth ID + Company Name, repack securely in Shenzhen, and consolidate into a single international courier shipment (`DHL/FedEx/UPS`) with itemized tracking.
