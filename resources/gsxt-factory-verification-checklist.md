# China Factory Verification & GSXT Due Diligence Checklist

Standard due diligence and pre-shipment verification framework before transferring production deposits or releasing final export balances.

See the full redacted verification file: https://chinawithme.com/samples/factory-verification-report  
Compute ISO 2859-1 sample plans: https://chinawithme.com/tools/aql-calculator

## 1. Official Registry & Legal Verification (`gsxt.gov.cn`)
- [ ] Verify exact Chinese registered entity name (公司全称) and 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code.
- [ ] Check operating status (`存续` / Active), registration date, and paid-in capital vs. subscribed capital.
- [ ] Inspect approved Business Scope (`经营范围`) to confirm direct manufacturing vs. wholesale/trading only.
- [ ] Query PRC court judgment and enforcement lists for unpaid supplier lawsuits, wage arrears, or frozen assets.
- [ ] Verify Proforma Invoice bank beneficiary matches the verified mainland PRC corporate name character-for-character (flag unexplained third-party HK shell or personal bank transfers).

## 2. On-Site Factory Floor Audit (Non-Outsourced Capability Check)
- [ ] Match factory gate signage and license wall plaque against registered GSXT address.
- [ ] Inspect active production machinery (SMT pick-and-place lines, CNC spindles, injection molding tonnage) vs. idle or staged demo machines.
- [ ] Confirm in-house QC bench equipment calibration stickers (calipers, hi-pot testers, pull gauges, metallurgical test kits).
- [ ] Check whether critical sub-processes (anodizing, plating, surface mount, packaging) remain in-house or are subcontracted externally.

## 3. ISO 2859-1 (ANSI/ASQ Z1.4) Pre-Shipment Inspection Protocol
- [ ] Require minimum 80%–100% export-packed batch completion before sampling.
- [ ] Pull random sealed master cartons proportionally across **bottom, middle, and top rows** (never rely solely on factory-opened top-layer samples).
- [ ] Execute functional, safety, alloy/resin, carton drop, and barcode verification checks against AQL thresholds (commonly Critical `0`, Major `2.5`, Minor `4.0`).
- [ ] Seal passed Golden Sample cartons and archive continuous gate-entry photo + timestamp metadata before releasing final wire balance.
